# Brief B2. Growth & Sales: West Midlands landlord-certificate businesses (prospect list)
Read run/STATE.md (rules). Research only: do NOT contact anyone, submit forms, sign up or touch LinkedIn. Do your own fetching (at most 2 helpers). Never guess an email address.

WHY: under the draft regulations published on 9 September 2026, landlords in the West Midlands must register every property on the PRS Database from 15 December 2026. They also declare their gas safety record, EICR and EPC, and each record must be refreshed within 28 days of a renewal. Firms that ISSUE those certificates (gas engineers, electricians, EPC assessors) could give their landlord customers a branded two-page guide, which we sell as a white-label pack. That gives landlords a reason to go back to the firm at each renewal.

FIND 30 businesses in the West Midlands region (West Midlands county, Staffordshire, Warwickshire, Worcestershire, Shropshire, Herefordshire) that:
 (a) sell landlord certificates as a named service on their own site: EICR / electrical testing for landlords, landlord gas safety certificates (CP12), EPC assessments, or "landlord certificates" bundles (quote the sentence);
 (b) are a Ltd/LLP (evidence on their own site: "Ltd"/"Limited"/company number; or site names the company and Companies House confirms it is active);
 (c) publish a GENERIC business email on their own website (an info, hello, office, bookings or enquiries mailbox). Copy it exactly and record the page URL; skip gmail/hotmail-only businesses and personal named addresses;
 (d) are small/independent (owner reads the inbox). Max 5 per town. Skip any business already in run/prospects/partners.csv, agents.csv or westmids.csv.
CSV columns: company_name, short_name, website, email, email_source_url, town, service_type (EICR|gas|EPC|mixed), legal_form_evidence, service_evidence_quote, service_evidence_url, hook_sentence, hook_supporting_quote, saturday_open, notes.
 - short_name: natural trading name for a greeting.
 - hook_sentence: ONE sentence (max 24 words), second person, plain, factual, directly supported by hook_supporting_quote (verbatim from their site). Start with "I saw", "Your site says", "Your ... page says" or "You ...". No flattery, no claims of having used them, no exclamation marks, no dashes.
Machine-check that every email and quote appears on the cited page before you finish.
OUTPUT: run/prospects/wm_trades.csv (+ wm_trades_notes.md, max 40 short lines). Stop at 30 verified or ~40 minutes.
FINAL REPLY (max 12 lines): count, file path, split by service type, concerns.
